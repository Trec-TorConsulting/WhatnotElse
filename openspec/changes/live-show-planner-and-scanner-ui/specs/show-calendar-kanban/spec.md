## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Interactive Show Calendar View
The system SHALL configure a Frappe Desk Calendar view for `Whatnot Show` mapping start time, end time, show title, and status badges.

#### Scenario: View shows in calendar view
- **WHEN** a seller navigates to the Whatnot Show Calendar view in Frappe Desk
- **THEN** all scheduled, live, and completed shows render at their respective times with distinct color states.
